Farm transparency
The EcoKuku journey
From egg to your table — every step, no secrets.
Incubation — Day 1
Fertilised eggs are carefully placed in climate-controlled incubators at 37.5°C with 55–60% humidity. We monitor temperature and turning every 6 hours for 21 days.
Hatching — Day 21
Chicks hatch in our hatchery, immediately assessed for health. Only strong, active chicks are introduced to our growing houses. Typical hatch rate: 92–96%.
Growing phase — Week 1–3
Chicks are housed in ventilated, warm brooder houses. Fed organic starter mash high in protein. Growth is tracked weekly. No growth hormones — ever.
Organic feeding — Week 2–6
Transition to our certified organic grower mix — locally sourced maize, sunflower meal, and mineral supplements. Feed records are maintained batch by batch.
Health & vaccination
All birds follow a strict vaccination schedule — Newcastle disease, Gumboro, Marek's. A certified vet visits bi-weekly. Disease logs are kept for every batch.
Ready for market — Week 6–8
Broilers reach 1.8–2.2kg live weight. Processing is hygienic, KEBS-compliant, and traceable. Packaged within 4 hours of processing and delivered chilled.
Our farm in Ruiru, Kiambu County
5 acres of free-range land. 8 housing units. Visited and certified annually.
